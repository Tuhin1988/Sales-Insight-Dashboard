# 📊 Power BI Sales Insight Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to deliver comprehensive sales performance analysis across products, regions, and time periods. It enables stakeholders to monitor key metrics, identify growth trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Track critical KPIs such as Total Sales, Gross Profit, Profit Margin, and Quantity Sold
* Analyze sales performance across product categories and brands
* Identify top-performing products and revenue contributors
* Monitor month-over-month sales trends and growth rates
* Provide sales forecasting for upcoming periods

## 📈 Key Insights

* **Total Sales reached 1.407B** with **Gross Profit of 576M** and **~40.96% Profit Margin**
* **COGS accounts for 831M**, indicating a significant cost component impacting profitability
* **Top-performing product category: Camera (388M)** followed by Monitor and Scanner
* **Samsung and Dell** are the leading contributors in total sales by brand
* **Sales are geographically distributed** across multiple regions in Bangladesh, with key concentration around major cities
* **Monthly sales show fluctuations**, with peaks around mid-year and year-end periods
* **Month-over-Month growth varies significantly**, including both positive spikes (~10%) and declines (~-5%)
* **Pareto analysis shows a small number of products contribute to the majority of revenue (80/20 rule)**
* Forecast indicates relatively stable sales with slight upward trends in the next 3 months

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file
2. Open it using Power BI Desktop
3. Refresh the dataset if needed
4. Use slicers (Zone: North, South, Mid) and filters to explore insights dynamically
5. Analyze trends through visualizations such as Pareto chart, MoM growth, and forecast
